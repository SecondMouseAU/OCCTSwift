#2841 ground truth: the THREE-curve constructors of GeomFill_BSplineCurves and
GeomFill_BezierCurves, which of the three filling styles each accepts, and what the kernel we
ship does when the caller puts the wrong curve in the C2 slot.

Built and run 2026-09-30 against the PINNED asset, v4.0.0-kernel.3
(.build/artifacts/<pkg>/OCCT/OCCT.xcframework/macos-arm64), not a local Libraries/ build:

  clang++ -std=c++17 -ObjC++ -w -I"$XCF/macos-arm64/Headers" -L"$XCF/macos-arm64" -lOCCT-macos \
    -framework Foundation -framework AppKit -lz -lc++ Scripts/repro/2841/probe.mm -o probe

Each section runs as its own process (probe <A..G>) because three of them crash. Section G forks
a child per case instead, so the crashing cases can be counted rather than avoided.

WHAT IT SETTLES

1. C2 IS THE MIDDLE CURVE, POSITIONALLY, AND GETTING IT WRONG CRASHES.
   Both 3-curve Inits synthesise a straight two-pole fourth side between the FAR ends of C1 and
   C3, where "far" is the endpoint that does not meet C2 (GeomFill_BSplineCurves.cxx:396-439,
   GeomFill_BezierCurves.cxx:304-335), then call the 4-curve Init. Put a curve that is not
   adjacent to both others in the C2 slot and the synthesised side closes nothing: Arrange
   returns false, the refusal behind it is compiled out (#2842), and Init reads CC1->Degree()
   off a null handle. Sections [D] (BSpline) and [E] (Bezier) are exit 139, and so is [F], three
   curves that form no chain at all. This is why the bridge wrappers need the same guard the
   4-curve ones got in #2829, extended over the synthesised side.

2. THE DIRECTION OF EACH CURVE DOES NOT MATTER; ONLY WHICH ONE IS IN C2 DOES.
   Section [G] walks 3 middle-slot assignments x 8 direction flips, for both flavours, 48 cases.
   The 16 canonical-middle cases all build a surface and the 32 wrong-middle ones all crash the
   kernel. The far-end test is direction-independent by construction: it asks which endpoint of
   C1 is not within Precision::Confusion() of either endpoint of C2, and answers the same either
   way round. Arrange then handles the rest.

3. THE TRANSCRIBED GUARD AGREES WITH THE KERNEL ON ALL 48. Synthesise the chord's two endpoints
   with the kernel's own two tests (Tol = Precision::Confusion() squared, against SquareDistance,
   so the effective tolerance is 1e-7), then run the #2829 predicate over the four endpoint
   pairs. 48/48, and the 32 disagreements it would take to be wrong are exactly the 32 crashes.

4. .coons IS REACHABLE FOR BOTH FLAVOURS, WHICH #2841 LEFT OPEN.
   #2841 expected the synthesised degree-1 side to keep NbVPoles below 4 and put .coons out of
   reach for the BSpline flavour. It does not: the 4-curve Init raises degrees BEFORE aligning
   knot distributions (GeomFill_BSplineCurves.cxx:288-309), so the chord is raised to
   DegV = max(deg CC2, deg CC4) first. Section [A]: three degree-3 sides give a 4x4 Coons
   surface. What does put .coons out of reach is a low-degree MIDDLE curve, because then DegV is
   low too: a two-pole degree-1 C2 gives NbVPoles = 2 and a real, catchable
   Standard_ConstructionError "invalid filling style" (GeomFill_BSplineCurves.cxx:300), which
   the bridge's catch (...) already turns into nullptr. The Bezier flavour has no such limit at
   all, because its Coons branch raises every side to at least degree 3 first
   (GeomFill_BezierCurves.cxx:212-215): section [B] builds 4x4 from a two-pole middle.

5. A PERIODIC SUPPORT BEHAVES THE SAME WAY, per the #430 rule. Section [C] is a quarter patch on
   a cylinder of radius 5: bottom arc, seam, top arc, with the chord closing the second seam.
   .stretch and .curved build a 3x4 surface whose own bounds are u=[0,1.5708] v=[0,1], and
   .coons throws the same catchable "invalid filling style" because the arcs carry 3 poles.

6. THE SYNTHESISED SIDE IS A BOUNDARY OF THE RESULT, NOT A DEGENERATE CORNER.
   Section [A]'s square comes back with all four corners distinct: (uMin,vMin) = C1's far end,
   (uMax,vMin) = C1's near end, and the chord runs along the U = first edge. A 3-curve fill is a
   three-sided boundary closed with a straight chord, not a triangular patch.
