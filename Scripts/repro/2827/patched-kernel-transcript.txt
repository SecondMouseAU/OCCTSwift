transcript establishes, each of them an identity the regression suite asserts rather than a number
it copies:

  1. the by-plane per-face sum equals the solid's volume, for every plane, matching
     BRepGProp::VolumeProperties (a separate loop) and VolumePropertiesGK (a separate integrator);
  2. the mass-weighted sum of the per-face gravity centres equals the solid's first moment, for
     every plane, including on a fixture translated off the origin so the comparison is not against
     (0, 0, 0);
  3. per face, the mass is affine in the plane offset with slope the face's signed projected area:
     +-371.72566611769 on the plate's two caps, which is +-their area, and 0 on the five faces
     parallel to the plane normal.

distance, and SetLocation does not re-base it. mass / area reads d1 off a flat cap directly, and it
answers 2, 3 and -98 for planes at z = 0, 1 and -100 against a cap at z = 2, identically for loc at
the origin and at z = 3.
