Read the four blocks in order:

  1. `Vinert d1` and `VinertGK d1` are equal in every row, so OCCT's two by-plane implementations
     agree with each other. BRepGProp_Vinert integrates through BRepGProp_Gauss, BRepGProp_VinertGK
     through math_KronrodSingleIntegration and BRepGProp_UFunction; they share no code, and both
     convert the gp_Pln the same way (BRepGProp_Vinert.cxx:279, BRepGProp_VinertGK.cxx:219 and
     :244, both `aCoeff[3] = d - n . loc`, both consumed with a minus at BRepGProp_Gauss.cxx:343
     and BRepGProp_UFunction.cxx:99). This is a second construction, not a re-run of the first.
  2. Both disagree with the gp_Pln they were handed. `gp_Pln n.C+d` is the plane's own signed
     distance to the cap's area centroid, computed from gp_Pln::Coefficients, and it matches the
     `geometric` column and not the two measured ones. `d1` is its mirror through the origin:
     2 against 2, 3 against 1, -98 against 102, 7 against -3.
  3. SetLocation does not move `d1`, at the origin, at (0, 0, 3) and at the non-axial (-4, 11, 2.5).
     That is the correct behaviour and not a second defect: the distance from a plane to a point is
     not a function of the moment reference, and the `- n . loc` re-basing exists precisely to
     cancel the `P - loc` the integrand works in. It cancels exactly, both today and after the
     sign is corrected.
  4. Handing the kernel the plane mirrored through the origin, gp_Pln(gp_Pnt(n * -offset), n),
     makes `d1` the geometric signed distance to the plane at `offset`, for both implementations
     and all three locations. That is the bridge-side fix in OCCTBRepGPropVinertPlane, measured
     before it was written.

The column centroid block is the same conclusion in a form that needs no closed form: the cap is at
z = 2, so the column between it and the plane at z = p has its centroid at z = (2 + p) / 2. Passing
the plane as-is puts the centroid at 0.5 for offset 1, which is the midpoint to z = -1. Mirrored, it
is 1.5, the midpoint to z = 1.

The last block re-measures #2827's two aggregate identities under the mirrored plane, on a fixture
translated off the origin so the first moment is not (0, 0, 0): the per-face sum is still
BRepGProp::VolumeProperties' volume and the mass-weighted centre sum is still its first moment, for
a plane through the origin, one 100 away and an oblique one. Both identities need only that the
weight is affine with gradient n, which mirroring preserves, so the fix moves the per-face
decomposition and leaves the totals untouched.
